Open Observatory

Tracking data-centre construction and activity from free satellite data, with sources shown for every claim.

We can see construction. Sentinel-2 dates every hall's roof to the month and shows fit-out afterwards.
We can see fuel burning on site. Where a site runs its own turbines, NO₂ plumes give a monthly activity readout, calibrated against EPA-monitored power plants.
We cannot see electricity use directly. Roof temperature does not track load, day or night (tested at 12 sites). Load figures below are documented capacity with a stated assumption, unless marked as measured.
