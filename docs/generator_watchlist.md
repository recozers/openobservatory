## Monthly screen and refresh

`python tools/generator_watchlist.py` replays saved TROPOMI daily profiles. `python tools/generator_watchlist.py --live` uses the existing noninteractive Earth Engine authentication helper to extract the latest **three complete calendar months**, replacing that overlap while preserving earlier observations and the pre-cut baseline. First extraction starts at the registered `profile_start`. Inputs are COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_NO2 and ERA5 100 m winds; bins −12 to +30 km, crosswind half-width 12 km, cloud fraction ≤0.3, wind 2–12 m/s, 19–21 UTC. Both current watches are in the US. Other longitudes need local-overpass wind alignment before registration.

`data/refresh_flux_sources.csv` explicitly registers both watches with `refresh_mode=generator_watch`. `tools/refresh.py` calls this pipeline in live mode only when its existing credential checks allow a live refresh; dry runs make no new EE requests. Existing non-watch flux sources retain their prior replay behavior. The workflow saves raw watch profiles, extraction extent, monthly series, audit, first-detection ledger and log alongside public JSON. A green dry run does not establish working CI service-account credentials; these manual initial extractions used already-authorised local OAuth. The local `.env` references a service-account file absent from this worktree; it was not silently repaired or copied into the repository.

The frozen seasonal baseline is **2024–2025**, with an analysis cutoff of **1 January 2026** for both watches. It is not a first-fire date. For each complete post-cut month, subtract the corresponding calendar-month baseline mean from the daily near-source-minus-upwind plateau. Require at least three valid daily observations in both the target month and the pooled baseline month. Keep signed negative changes and omit unobservable months rather than filling zeros.

For calibrated conversion factor `F`, monthly standard error is:

`sqrt(F² × (post_variance / post_days + baseline_variance / baseline_days) + (abs(rate) × calibration_scatter / calibration_factor)²)`

Unlike the legacy monthly converter, this includes uncertainty in the estimated baseline. It retains the existing tall-coal-stack calibration; gas-stack transfer, spatial source separation, autocorrelation and repeated testing remain unvalidated. Quarter plots conservatively average monthly errors rather than claiming independent calibration errors. Only monthly results decide the watch state.

The map starts at **“watching: generator fleet under construction”**. A finite positive monthly change **strictly greater than 2σ and 100 kg/h** turns it orange, labelled as measured NOx change with unconfirmed fleet attribution. This is a screening alert, not proof that the named fleet fired, not a commissioning date and not a multiple-testing-adjusted discovery. Targets in the UI retain their original year/half-year precision. Missing emission factors or permit ceilings never fall back to an uncontrolled gas prior for these sites; NOx-to-generation and campus-MW inversion remain disabled even after a crossing.

The first crossing per site appends one entry to `results_no2/generator_watch/detections.csv` and `docs/LOG.md`; replay is idempotent. The ledger preserves that historical notification if later data revisions remove the crossing, while the public colour always follows current saved evidence and `audit.json`. No external message is sent.

Largest positive monthly z is about **1.43** (March 2026, 313.0 ±218.9 kg/h). The full-period EMG fit is background-inclusive and is not used as a commissioning detection.
