# Municipal monthly water records — B4

Public-source audit, 13 September 2026. **One attributable campus series found among eight city searches:** Bluffdale's deliveries to the Utah Data Center. The CSV has 312 observations across four distinct reporting series: 120 city delivery months, 120 customer purchase months, 60 irrigation-transfer months and 12 river-return months. These are not 312 independent delivery months or four campuses.

## What is displayed

The [Bluffdale Water System report](https://www.waterrights.utah.gov/asp_apps/viewEditPWS/pwsView.asp?SYSTEM_ID=1264#108544372) identifies deliveries to Utah Data Center, linked reciprocally to the [customer's report](https://www.waterrights.utah.gov/asp_apps/viewEditPWS/pwsView.asp?SYSTEM_ID=11418#108544468). This matches existing inventory site `nsa_utah`. These are local system filings hosted by the Utah Division of Water Rights, not national statistics.

The main monthly strip uses city deliveries (2016–2025). The customer purchase meter is a separate comparison, never added to deliveries. Outbound irrigation transfers (2021–2025) and river returns (2025 only) remain separate; missing discharge records prevent a defensible net-consumption series. 2022 irrigation values are labelled Calculated by the reporting entity. 2016 customer measurement method is not stated.

The two intake reports differ: October 2023 is 29.97 acre-feet in the city report and 32.01 in the customer report. In 2020, annual values are 450.10 versus 464.68 acre-feet. Customer notes flag meter/EMCS issues and chiller cleaning/flushing that year. City notes describe a corrected April 2021 reading and planned meter relocation/recalibration. They are warnings about measurement comparability, not inferred workload changes. The annual 2025 delivery total is 422.14 acre-feet; displayed months sum to 422.13 because of rounding.

No water observation changes an electricity estimate, operating verdict or evidence colour. Water volume depends on weather, cooling design, cleaning and other uses; no monthly WUE or electricity conversion is asserted. The UI identifies the last reported month, displays gaps as unknown and provides separate meter/flow values. No 2026 observations are invented.

## Reproduction and checks

`data/water_sources/manifest.json` records source URLs, record IDs, retrieval date, original-response SHA-256 and extracted-table SHA-256. Four HTML files contain only the relevant numeric tables. Source comments are summarised above and in manifest notes. Original full response caches are ignored; public contact details are not copied into the extracts.

`python tools/water_monthly.py --rebuild` reproduces `data/water_monthly.csv` from these table extracts. `python tools/water_monthly.py` validates the CSV and is called by the monthly refresh. The build is offline; fetching/reviewing an updated public filing is separate from replaying an existing snapshot. `extract_table(document, record_id)` selects each source's monthly table for a future reviewed update; update both source and table hashes together.

Reported units remain acre-feet. The display uses megalitres: 1 acre-foot = 1.23348183754752 ML using the international foot. Decimal arithmetic precedes rounding to six decimal places; input precision is only 0.01 acre-foot. Annual reconciliation allows 0.065 acre-foot, the maximum accumulated rounding from twelve two-decimal monthly values and one annual value. Blank/-- remains missing; zero stays zero. Negative/nonfinite volumes, wrong units, malformed/duplicate months, bad conversions and multiple preferred intake records are rejected. Source/customer differences are preserved, not corrected by averaging.
