## Reproducible flux result

Run `python tools/onsite_generation.py --replay`, then `python tools/refresh.py --dry-run`.

The replay calls the existing `tools/no2_flux.py` composite estimator on saved daily profiles, then `tools/no2_flux_quarterly.py`'s monthly method with the current calibration. It makes no new Earth Engine query. The inputs already extend through August 2026; file hashes, observation counts, calibration hash and output hashes are recorded in `results_no2/onsite_generation_audit.json`. Fresh satellite extraction remains `tools/no2_flux.py --name ... --lat ... --lon ... --start ... --end ... --out ...`, one site per process with a cached output.

- **Abilene:** 880 valid daily profiles, 2023-01-01–2026-08-31. Seasonal baseline before 2025-01-01 is an analysis assumption, not a first-fire date. None of 20 post-baseline months reaches 2.5σ; maximum 2.381. Verdict: **monthly flux below detection**. The separate site's plume-change test can detect combustion on another aggregation; this result does not mean the campus is idle.
- **Colossus 2:** 607 daily profiles, 2023-01-01–2026-08-28. Existing baseline before 2025-07-01 retained. Seven of 14 post-baseline months reach 2.5σ; maximum 4.386. Verdict: **existing monthly signal detected**, not a newly measured site or a verified commissioning date for the permanent SCR fleet.
- **Colossus 1:** Claude's C2 subtraction remains 62 ± 63 kg/h for the turbine-period step, below detection. Other register entries lack verified operating generation to target; no synthetic monthly series was filled in.

These monthly screens inherit the current method's conditional baseline uncertainty and tall-stack coal calibration. They are descriptive screens, not multiple-testing-adjusted discoveries or proof of campus-specific generation. The full-period composite includes the baseline and must not be read as a turbine-period increase. Negative months stay negative. Air-permit ceilings do not identify actual emission factors or utilisation.

`nox_ef_basis=permit_upper_limit` disables NOx-to-MW inversion while preserving the NOx evidence strip. Abilene's lower factor is blank. Its existing documented-capacity estimate is retained; no gas prior is silently substituted for its SCR fleet. The existing Colossus 2 factor range is unchanged and remains a modelling uncertainty, not a permit measurement.
